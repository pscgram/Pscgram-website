Cancellation & Refund Policy

Policy for cancellations, failed delivery and refunds of PSCGram digital products.

1. Digital-product policy

PSCGram sells digital study materials. Because digital content can be accessed immediately after successful payment, cancellation after successful delivery is generally not available.

2. When you may request a refund

A refund may be considered when a payment is successfully captured but the purchased digital product cannot be delivered, the delivered file is materially corrupted/unusable, or a duplicate payment has been made for the same order.

3. Failed or reversed payments

If a payment is failed, cancelled, or automatically reversed by the payment gateway, no product delivery is required for that transaction. If your bank account was debited despite a failed status, the amount is normally handled through the payment gateway/banking reversal process. Contact us if the amount does not return within the expected period.

4. How to request support/refund

Email pscgramonline@gmail.com with your name, email, payment/order ID, date, amount and a brief description of the issue. Do not send card PINs, UPI PINs, passwords or secret keys.

5. Review process

PSCGram will review the transaction and delivery status. Where a refund is approved, it will be initiated through the applicable payment/refund process. Processing time can depend on the payment gateway and the customer's bank.

6. Online exams

Online exam attempts are digital services. Any exam-specific cancellation or refund terms shown at the time of purchase, if applicable, will also apply.

7. Contact

Email: pscgramonline@gmail.com
