Privacy Policy

Payments are processed by Razorpay. PSCGram does not ask you to enter your card or UPI credentials directly into PSCGram pages. Payment details are handled by the payment gateway according to its policies.

3. How information is used

PSCGram may use trusted service providers required to operate the website, such as payment processing, hosting, database, authentication and secure file-storage services. Information is shared only as reasonably necessary to provide the requested service or comply with applicable law.

5. Cookies and technical data

The website may use essential browser storage, cookies or similar technologies for functionality, authentication, security and service operation. Your browser may also provide standard technical information such as device and browser details.

6. Data security

PSCGram uses reasonable technical measures to protect information and digital files. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for transactions, support, security, legal obligations and legitimate business records.

8. Your choices

For questions about personal information or a purchase record, contact pscgramonline@gmail.com.

9. Policy updates

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with the updated version.
